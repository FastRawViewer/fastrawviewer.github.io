How It Works

Step 1: Load Your RAWs

Open any folder. FastRawViewer instantly decodes and displays thumbnails and previews for every supported format.

Step 2: Cull and Rate

Scan at speed. Assign stars, labels, or reject with keyboard shortcuts. Filters narrow to focus on priorities.
